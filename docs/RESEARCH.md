# Why small businesses hesitate on AI: research library

Checked Nov 8, 2026. Every quote is word for word and kept short. Rows without a quote give a *research insight*: a finding paraphrased, not a quotation. **None of the people quoted are MainStreet AI customers.** The homepage shows a subset, from `data/research/painpoints.json`.

## Headline findings

- Owners planning to use AI struggle most with **finding tools that meet their needs (54%)**, then **time to set up or train staff (37%)**. Fed, 2025 SBCS, employer firms, n=810.
- Owners with no plans mostly say **AI isn't applicable to their business (56%)** or **prefer not to use it (30%)**. n=1,855.
- Owners already using AI name **accuracy (46%)** and **adapting tools to their business (43%)** as top challenges. n=2,203.
- Use is shallow: **49% of AI users are experimenting, 7% fully integrated** (Fed). In Goldman Sachs' survey, **76% use AI but only 14% have it in core operations**, and **73% want training and implementation help** (n=1,256).
- Adoption figures disagree (8.8% to 89%) because surveys define "using AI" differently, so we don't put a single adoption number on the homepage.

## Other sources

- [ICIC, AI in Business](https://icic.org/research/ai-in-business): 3,700+ owners, five focus groups; owners prefer hands-on, flexible learning.
- [US Chamber, Empowering Small Business 2025](https://www.uschamber.com/technology/empowering-small-business-the-impact-of-technology-on-u-s-small-business?state=tx): 58% use generative AI; 65% worry about a patchwork of state AI laws.
- [SBA Office of Advocacy, Sep 24, 2025](https://advocacy.sba.gov/2025/09/24/new-advocacy-article-highlights-small-businesses-closing-the-ai-adoption-gap/): Census BTOS puts small-firm AI use at 8.8%.
- [SF Fed qualitative brief (PDF)](https://www.frbsf.org/wp-content/uploads/ai-adoption-among-small-businesses-cdrb202601.pdf): 13,608 firms answered the 2024 SBCS; 8,500+ answered the open-ended AI question.

## What we did with it

| Pain point | Product answer |
| --- | --- |
| Don't know where to start | Start from the business name; we read what's public |
| Not for my business | Problems found in their own reviews and website, with proof; "you don't need AI for this" when true |
| No time | About 3 minutes; up to 4 skippable questions; 30-day plan with minutes per step |
| Wrong answers | The advisor answers only from results and the checked catalog; AI wording is checked against those facts |
| Data and privacy | Privacy answers on every tool; we never log into owners' accounts |
| Personal touch | "What stays human" on every example and answer |
| Cost | Real starting prices, budget plans, free fixes first, "price not public" said out loud |
| AI already in their tools | "Use what you already have" ranks first |
| Stuck experimenting | 30-day plan, day-30 check, free SBDC advisor |
